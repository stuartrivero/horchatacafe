A traditional food from England used in a Sunday Roast

Ingredients

- 140g [plain flour](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/glossary/flour-glossary)(this is about 200ml/7fl oz)
- 4 [eggs](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/glossary/egg-glossary)(200ml/7fl oz)
- 200ml [milk](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/glossary/milk-glossary)
- [sunflower oil](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/glossary/sunflower-oil-glossary)for cooking

Method

- Step 1  
  Heat oven to 230C/fan 210C/gas 8.
- Step 2  
  Drizzle a little sunflower oil evenly into two [4-hole Yorkshire pudding tins](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/review/best-yorkshire-pudding-tins) or two 12-hole non-stick muffin tins and place in the oven to heat through.
- Step 3  
  To make the batter, tip 140g plain flour into a bowl and [beat](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/review/best-whisks) in 4 eggs until smooth.
- Step 4  
  Gradually add 200ml milk and carry on beating until the mix is completely lump-free. Season with salt and pepper.
- Step 5  
  Pour the batter into a jug, then remove the hot tins from the oven. Carefully and evenly pour the batter into the holes.
- Step 6  
  Place the tins back in the oven and leave undisturbed for 20-25 mins until the puddings have puffed up and browned.
- Step 7  
  Serve immediately. You can now cool them and freeze for up to 1 month.

Excellent drowned in gravy or in syrup for dessert!
